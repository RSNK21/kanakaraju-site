About C. Kanakaraju

A banker who reads poetry, and a poetry lover who reads balance sheets.

C. Kanakaraju spent more than 25 years with Union Bank of India and retired voluntarily as Assistant General Manager. He headed branches in Mysuru, Pune and Bylakuppe, where he opened the bank's branch for the Tibetan settlement and was commended by the MD & CEO.

As Deputy Regional Head in Thiruvananthapuram he looked after 50 branches. At the MSME Loan Point in Bengaluru and Coimbatore he sanctioned more than ₹250 crore of MSME loans in 2023-24 and ran camps to bring small businesses to the bank. Between 2016 and 2020 he taught at the bank's Staff Training College in Mangaluru.

When branches went through hard years, he went back to basics: meet the customer, understand the need, fix the problem. Behind every loan file is a family, a shop, or a fee that must be paid by June. That is still how he works.

Kanaka: the gold of culture

Mysuru taught us that money and art belong in the same room.

The old Mysuru court paid its painters, poets and musicians as seriously as its treasurers. Gold leaf on a Mysuru painting and a well-kept ledger come from the same habit of care.

D.V. Gundappa wrote about living with balance and honesty, and many Kannadigas carry his Mankuthimmana Kagga in their heads. Here that thinking shapes how money is treated: as a means, never the master.
